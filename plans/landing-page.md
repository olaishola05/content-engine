# Landing Page Plan: V1 High-Converting Homepage

## Overview
Build a state-of-the-art, high-converting Landing Page for ContentEngine at `/` (replacing the placeholder `src/app/page.tsx`). The landing page communicates the core product positioning: "One Input. Full Content Pack. Your Voice. Every Platform."

## Core Sections & Components

1. **Header Navigation**:
   - Logo + Brand Name (`ContentEngine`)
   - Links: Features, How It Works, Outputs, Security, FAQ
   - Action Buttons: `Sign In` / `Get Started →`

2. **Hero Section**:
   - Beta Badge: `✨ Private Beta — Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) Access`
   - H1: "Turn One Idea Into a Full Branded Content Pack Across Every Platform"
   - Subheadline: "Transform transcripts, blogs, or quick thoughts into platform-native posts, long-form SEO articles, visual carousels, and impact cards — matched to your unique brand voice."
   - CTAs: `Start Repurposing Free →` (links to `/sign-up` or `/dashboard`) and `Watch Demo / How It Works`
   - Interactive Live Demo Preview Component (Tabbed output mockup: X Thread, LinkedIn, Carousel PDF, Impact Card)

3. **Metrics & Value Highlights Banner**:
   - `5 Output Formats` • `3 Variations per Platform` • `100% Brand Calibrated` • `1-Click PDF Export`

4. **"Before vs. After" Comparison Section**:
   - Direct side-by-side comparison: Manual Repurposing (4+ hours, generic voice, weak hooks) vs. ContentEngine (60 seconds, 100% on-brand, hook-scored, 1-click PDF/PNG).

5. **Brand Voice Ingestion Visual Demo**:
   - Interactive card showing document upload (PDF/DOCX) -> structured extraction of Niche, Target Audience, Tone, and Content Pillars.

6. **Multi-Platform Supported Formats Grid**:
   - Interactive badges & cards for X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube SEO, and Long-Form Blogs.

7. **BYOK Security & Privacy Section**:
   - AES-256 encrypted API key privacy guarantees for testers.

8. **Interactive FAQ Accordion**:
   - Accordion answering BYOK security, brand voice learning, PDF exports, and supported platforms.

9. **Footer**:
   - Product links, copyright, and brand links.
